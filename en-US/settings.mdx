Open **Settings** in the sidebar. The navigation shows only the sections that the current workspace
and the permissions of the account allow. Search the sections with the box at the top of the
navigation.

Most sections belong to the current workspace. A few apply to the whole instance and appear only in
the protected `_sys` organization; they are marked `_sys` below.

## Current organization

| Section | Purpose | Permission | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| **General** | Name, identifiers, preference defaults, registration, and public sharing. | `org.preferences.read` / `org.preferences.manage`; `org.sharing.read` / `org.sharing.manage` for public sharing | [General settings](/en-US/settings/organization) |

## Notify

| Section | Purpose | Permission | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Connectors** | Email, IM, phone and SMS, and webhook connectors with encrypted credentials. | `notify.connectors.read` / `notify.connectors.manage` | [Notification channels](/en-US/notification-channels#create-and-test-a-connector) |
| **User routing** | The notification methods and delivery order of each member. | `org.members.read` / `org.members.update` for other members | [Bind user notification methods](/en-US/notification-channels#bind-user-notification-methods) |
| **Policies** | Match events, resolve recipients, and choose the delivery. | `notify.policies.read` / `notify.policies.manage` | [Create templates and policies](/en-US/notification-channels#create-templates-and-policies) |
| **Templates** | Reusable notification messages with a preview, sorted by channel. | `notify.templates.read` / `notify.templates.manage` | [Create templates and policies](/en-US/notification-channels#create-templates-and-policies) |
| **Fallback routes** | Team and organization routes by notification category. | `notify.defaults.read` / `notify.defaults.manage` | [Configure fallback routes](/en-US/notification-channels#configure-fallback-routes) |
| **Delivery log** | Delivery chains, acknowledgement, and retries. | `notify.deliveries.read`, `notify.deliveries.acknowledge`, or `notify.deliveries.retry` by action | [Inspect and retry deliveries](/en-US/notification-channels#inspect-and-retry-deliveries) |
