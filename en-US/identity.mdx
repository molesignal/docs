MoleSignal authorizes actions with permission keys, not by comparing role names. A user can belong to
several organizations and hold different permissions in each one. Open **Identity & Access** in the
sidebar to manage the access of the current workspace.

## How authorization works

For an authenticated request, MoleSignal resolves:

1. the subject: a user, a service account, or an API token;
2. the active organization, or the protected platform scope;
3. the role assignments and the access grants of the subject;
4. the resource relationships and the accepted cross-organization grants;
5. the permission that the route and the target resource require.

The permission catalog, the built-in roles, and the permission policies are stored in PostgreSQL.
`GET /api/v1/iam/capabilities` returns the effective permissions that the web application uses to
build its navigation.

<Warning>
  A visible page or a role label is not authorization. The server checks the exact permission and
  the ownership of the resource for every protected operation.
</Warning>

## Permission scopes

Organization permissions cover one workspace. Examples:

- `org.preferences.read` and `org.preferences.manage`;
- `org.members.read` and `org.members.update`;
- `iam.roles.read`, `iam.roles.manage`, `iam.grants.read`, and `iam.grants.manage`;
- the permissions of streams, dashboards, alerts, schedules, saved views, pipelines, functions,
  reports, audit, log inspections, and Mole Agent.

Platform permissions begin with `sys.` and apply only in the `_sys` organization. They control the
organizations of the instance, system telemetry, trace debugging, platform administrators, and
platform settings.

## Built-in roles

| Role | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Every organization permission of the catalog, and nothing of the platform's. |
| **Admin** | Every organization permission: the product areas, and also settings, members, roles, access grants, tokens, audit, status pages, synthetics, service levels, postmortems, and the administration of Mole Agent. |
| **Editor** | Reads and changes streams, dashboards, alerts and schedules, saved views, pipelines, functions, reports, and the APM and RUM settings, and runs Mole Agent automations and log inspections. |
| **Viewer** | Reads data, dashboards, alerts and schedules, saved views, pipelines, functions, and reports, and chats with Mole Agent. |

The [permission reference](/en-US/identity/roles#permission-reference) lists every permission with
the built-in roles that hold it. Built-in roles cannot be edited. A permission that an upgrade adds
reaches the Owner role at startup.

Create a [custom role](/en-US/identity/roles#roles) for a narrower responsibility.

## Platform access

The `root` account configured for the instance is the super administrator. It needs no role: in any
organization it holds that organization's permissions, in `_sys` it holds the platform's, and it is
not the Owner of anything. Its API tokens hold the organization permissions of the organization they
belong to, and never a platform permission.

Nobody else reaches the platform unless granted. In `_sys`, **Identity & Access** has its own
**Roles**, **Permission policies**, and **Access grants**, which take `sys.administrators.read` and
`sys.administrators.manage`. Two platform roles are built in:

| Role | Holds |
|---|---|
| `platform_admin` | Every platform permission. |
| `platform_viewer` | Every platform permission that only reads. |

A grant goes to an account, found by its email, which need not belong to any organization. It covers
the whole platform, or names one organization: then only `sys.organizations.read`, `.update`,
`.disable`, and `.delete` count, and only on that organization. Nobody grants what they do not hold
across the platform, and removing a grant ends the access at the next request.

## Cross-workspace access

An access grant can relate a subject in one organization to a resource in another. The server checks
that relationship and the resource permission together, and a grant never makes the other
organization the active workspace of the subject. See
[Share resources across organizations](/en-US/identity/roles#share-resources-across-organizations).

Dashboards and reports also have [resource sharing](/en-US/resource-sharing). A public share
receives a restricted share session, not a membership.

## Platform administration

The `_sys` organization is separated from organization IAM. A tenant session and an `ms_*` token
cannot list system telemetry or platform administrators. Use a session in `_sys` with the required
`sys.*` permission. See [Administration overview](/en-US/administration).
