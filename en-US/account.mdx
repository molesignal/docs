**Account settings** hold what belongs to one person rather than to the workspace. Open the user
avatar in the top bar and choose **Account settings**, **Preferences**, or **Notify**. Every member
can change their own account settings; only **API tokens** needs a permission.

| Group | Section | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Personal settings | **Profile** | Display name, avatar, and a short description. |
| | **Preferences** | Theme, density, timezone, date format, start page, and keyboard shortcuts. |
| | **Notify** | Personal notification methods, delivery order, and quiet hours. |
| Security & access | **Security & sign-in** | Password reset and linked sign-in methods. |
| | **Active sessions** | The session of the current browser. |
| | **API tokens** | Your own API tokens, which act as you. |
| Workspace | **Workspace & identity** | Workspaces, roles, and identifiers for automation. |

## Profile

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Display name** | The name that other members see. |
| **Sign-in email** | Read only. An administrator or the identity provider manages it. |
| **About you** | Optional, up to 500 characters. |
| Avatar | **Change avatar** uploads a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image of up to 2 MB. **Remove** goes back to the initials. |

Select **Save changes** to keep the profile.

## Preferences

Personal preferences take precedence over the [defaults of the workspace](/en-US/settings/organization#preference-defaults).
A query page can still change the time display for the current investigation.

| Section | Field | Choices |
|---|---|---|
| Appearance | **Default theme** | **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**. |
| | **Interface density** | **Comfortable** or **Compact**. Compact tightens rows and spacing in tables, log lists, navigation, and settings, and leaves charts, code, and conversations as they are. |
| Time & region | **Default timezone** | **Browser timezone** or a named zone. Dashboards, logs, metrics, and traces show times in it. |
| | **Date & time format** | The date as `2026-07-27`, `2026/07/27`, `27/07/2026`, or `07/27/2026`, and the time in **24 hour** or **12 hour** form. |
| Startup & interaction | **Open after entering workspace** | **Home**, **Last visited page**, **Dashboards**, **Logs**, **Metrics**, **Traces**, **Alert incidents**, **Mole Agent**, or a specific dashboard. |
| | **Enable keyboard shortcuts** | Turns the [global shortcuts](/en-US/console#keyboard-shortcuts) on or off. |

Select **Save settings**. Leaving the page with unsaved changes asks whether to discard them. The
interface language is chosen under **Language** in the user menu, not here.

## Notify

**Notify** lists the methods that reach the member in the current workspace, and the order in
which MoleSignal tries them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bind a method">
    Select **Bind notify method**. Choose a **Connector** that an administrator configured under
    [Settings → Notify → Connectors](/en-US/notification-channels), and enter the
    **External identity**: an email address, a user ID of the IM platform, or a phone number with
    `+` and the country code for voice and SMS. Give it a **Label**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Select **Send test** on the method. A message that arrives proves that the connector accepts
    the identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Have it verified">
    A method delivers only when it is enabled and verified. An administrator with
    `org.members.update` selects **Verify** on it after confirming that the identity belongs to the
    member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose it for a category">
    Under **Delivery preferences**, turn on **Receive this category** for **Alert events**,
    **On-call events**, or **Reports**, and put the methods in order with **Move endpoint up** and
    **Move endpoint down**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Alert events** have two orders: **High-urgency alerts, in order** and **Low-urgency alerts, in
order**, used by the [urgency](/en-US/alerting/on-call#urgency) of the incident. Without low-urgency
steps, low urgency uses only the first high-urgency method. In each order, every step after the first can
wait a number of minutes before it is used. A later step reaches the member only when nobody has
acknowledged the alert by then. A step with the same minutes as the step before it is used only when that
step fails to send. See
[Move on when nobody acknowledges](/en-US/notification-channels#move-on-when-nobody-acknowledges).

**Use quiet hours** stops notifications of the category to the member between a **Start** and an
**End** time (`HH:MM`) in the chosen **Timezone**. They are not sent later. For alert events, low-urgency
alerts never come during quiet hours, and **Still get high-urgency alerts during quiet hours** lets
high-urgency alerts through. Remove a method from every delivery preference before deleting it.

Someone in an on-call schedule with no way to get alerts sees a notice here and on the on-call card of the
[home page](/en-US/home); see
[Members who cannot get alerts](/en-US/alerting/on-call#members-who-cannot-get-alerts).

The personal order is tried before the fallback routes of the team and the organization. See
[Notification channels and delivery](/en-US/notification-channels).

## Security and sessions

**Security & sign-in → Change password** sends a password reset link to the sign-in email. The
link works as described under [Sign in](/en-US/console#sign-in).

**Linked sign-in methods** lists the single sign-on identities that sign in to the account, with
their workspace and when each was last used. **Unlink** stops an identity from signing in to the
account; signing in with it again asks for the confirmation described under
[Link an existing account](/en-US/identity/sso#link-an-existing-account). An account without a
password keeps its last linked identity.

**Authenticator app** and **Passkeys** ask for one more check on top of the password, and a passkey can
also sign in without one. For setting them up, recovery codes, and what to do after losing a second factor,
see [Multi-factor authentication and passkeys](/en-US/identity/mfa).

**Active sessions** shows the session of the current browser. **Sign out this device** ends it;
**Sign out all other devices** ends every sign-in of the account in other browsers and on other
computers, and this browser stays signed in.

**API tokens** lists your own tokens and issues new ones with **New token**. A token acts as you and
never does more than you may at the time; its scope can narrow it further. This section needs
`personal_tokens.manage`, which every built-in role holds. See
[API tokens and service accounts](/en-US/identity/tokens).

## Workspace and identity

**Workspace & identity** lists the workspaces that the account belongs to, with its role and
status in each. **Switch** changes the current workspace. A workspace that a platform administrator
disabled cannot be opened.

**Advanced information** shows the **User ID**, the **Organization ID**, and the
**Organization slug** of the current workspace, each with **Copy**. Scripts, API calls, and
resource paths use them.
