MoleSignal is administered in three contexts. Each has its own pages and its own permissions.

| Context | Who | What | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal account | Every member, for their own account. | Profile, preferences, personal notifications, password, sessions, and memberships. | [Account settings](/en-US/account) |
| Workspace | Owners and admins of an organization. | Members, roles, access grants, tokens, single sign-on, workspace settings, notifications, data security, and audit. | [Identity & access](/en-US/identity), [Settings](/en-US/settings) |
| Platform `_sys` | Platform administrators, and the Owners of enabled organizations. | Organizations, instance-wide settings, system telemetry, and platform administrators. | [Platform settings](/en-US/settings/platform) |

## Workspace administration

A new workspace usually goes through these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Decide how people join">
    Set the [registration policy](/en-US/settings/organization#registration-and-access), and connect
    [single sign-on](/en-US/identity/sso) with the email domains of the company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give people the right access">
    Assign built-in roles to [members](/en-US/identity/members), create
    [custom roles](/en-US/identity/roles#roles) for narrower jobs, and group people into
    [teams](/en-US/identity/teams#teams) for on-call and ownership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give programs their own identity">
    Create [service accounts](/en-US/identity/tokens#service-accounts) for collectors, CI, and
    automation instead of personal tokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up notifications">
    Configure [connectors, policies, and fallback routes](/en-US/notification-channels) so that alerts
    and reports reach people.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protect the data">
    Add [regex patterns and field masking](/en-US/settings/data-security) for sensitive values, and
    review the [audit trail](/en-US/settings/data-security#audit) regularly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Platform administration

The permanent `_sys` organization is not a normal tenant. A session in `_sys` reaches only the pages
and routes that platform permissions allow. Platform administrators and the Owners of enabled
organizations can enter it. See [Built-in roles](/en-US/identity#built-in-roles).

| Permission | Allows |
|---|---|
| `sys.organizations.manage` | Create, disable, enable, and delete organizations. |
| `sys.settings.manage` | Change client IP recognition, default retention, metric cardinality, and audit retention. |
| `sys.telemetry.read`, `sys.telemetry.manage` | Read and configure the telemetry of MoleSignal itself. See [Self-observability](/en-US/self-observability). |
| `sys.trace_debug.manage` | Turn on and configure the trace debugging of the platform. |
| `sys.administrators.manage` | Add or remove platform administrators. |

Tenant sessions and `ms_*` API tokens cannot reach protected `_sys` data.
