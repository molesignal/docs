```http
GET /api/v1/streams/usage
```

Returns what each source took in over a window, and how each stands against its budget. Requires `intake_usage.read`, or `sys.telemetry.read` in the `_sys` organization.

A figure that is not known is `null`, not zero. When the records cannot be read, `available` is `false` and every figure is `null`. When metering began inside the window, `complete` is `false` and the figures are lower bounds.

**Query parameters**

| Param | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `dimension` | `credential` | `credential`, `stream`, or `service`. |
| `window_secs` | 86400 | Length of the window, limited to 3600 through 2678400. It starts on the hour. |

`source_key` reads `<credential_kind>:<id>` for a credential (`api_token`, `connector`, `user`, `internal`) and `<stream_type>:<name>` for a stream. For a service it is the name, with an empty string for events that name none and `*` for the services past the limit.

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl "http://localhost:5080/api/v1/streams/usage?dimension=credential&window_secs=86400" \
  -H "authorization: Bearer $MS_TOKEN"
```
</RequestExample>

## One source

```http
GET /api/v1/streams/usage/detail
```

Returns one source over the window: its totals, its hours gathered into buckets, and how it divides along the other two dimensions, at most 20 sources each.

| Param | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `dimension` | Required | `credential`, `stream`, or `service`. |
| `source_key` | Required | The source, as `GET /api/v1/streams/usage` gives it. |
| `window_secs` | 86400 | Limited to 3600 through 2678400. |
| `bucket_count` | 24 | Buckets in the series, limited to 6 through 48. |
