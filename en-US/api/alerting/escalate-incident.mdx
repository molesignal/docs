---
title: "Escalate an incident now"
description: "Notify a step of an incident's escalation policy at once."
"zh-Hans_link": "/zh-Hans/api/alerting/escalate-incident"
---

```http
POST /api/v1/alerts/incidents/{id}/escalate
```

Moves an active incident to a step of its escalation policy and notifies the targets of that step at once,
with `escalation.reason` set to `manual`. Without `step`, the next step that applies to the incident's
severity is taken. An `acknowledged` incident goes back to `open`, a snooze ends, and the step starts its
ack timeout; when nobody acknowledges, escalation goes on from there. The call requires
`alerts.incidents.escalate`, and the body may be empty.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `step` | integer | No | The step, counted from 0. It must apply to the incident's severity. Default: the next step that applies. |

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST http://localhost:5080/api/v1/alerts/incidents/$INCIDENT_ID/escalate \
  -H "authorization: Bearer $MS_TOKEN" \
  -H "content-type: application/json" \
  -d '{"step": 1}'
```
</RequestExample>

The response is the updated incident. [List incidents](/en-US/api/alerting/list-incidents) describes every field.

**Errors**

| Status | Cause |
|---|---|
| `400` | The incident has no escalation policy; `step` is beyond the policy's steps or does not apply to the incident's severity; or the incident is at its last step and no `step` was given. |
| `403` | The token lacks `alerts.incidents.escalate`, or the incident is out of the token's reach. |
| `404` | The incident does not exist. |
| `409` | The incident has recovered or was merged into another, or it kept changing while it was being updated; retry. |
