```http
GET /api/v1/alerts/insights
```

Aggregates the incidents that were created within a window, and how people responded to them: how soon
someone took them, how soon they recovered and how many escalated, against a period of the same length
before. Incidents merged into another are left out. The call requires `alerts.incidents.read`. Each person's on-call
load is in [On-call load](/en-US/api/alerting/oncall-load).

**Query parameters**

| Param | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `windowSecs` | integer | The look-back window in seconds. Default `604800` (7 days). The value is clamped to 3600 through 31536000. |

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl "http://localhost:5080/api/v1/alerts/insights?windowSecs=86400" \
  -H "authorization: Bearer $MS_TOKEN"
```

**Response**

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `windowSecs` | The window after clamping. |
| `total` | The number of incidents created in the window. |
| `active` | Incidents with the status `open` or `acknowledged`. |
| `closed` | Incidents with the status `resolved` or `closed`. |
| `mttrSecs` | The mean time in seconds from creation to resolution over the resolved incidents. `0` when none is resolved. |
| `noiseRate` | The fraction from 0 to 1 of resolved incidents that were resolved within 60 seconds. `0` when none is resolved. |
| `byHour` | 24 counts of incidents by the UTC hour of creation. |
| `bySeverity` | The count per severity. Severities without incidents are absent. |
| `topServices`, `topRules` | Up to 8 entries of `key` and `count`, highest first. The key is a service name or a rule identifier. |
| `response.current`, `response.previous` | How people responded in the window and in the period of the same length before it; the fields are below. |
| `response.breakdown` | The window split by team (`teams`, keyed by team ID), service (`services`) and person (`people`, keyed by the first person to take the incident), at most 20 rows each, most incidents first. An incident with several services counts once for each. Each row has `incidents`, the mean seconds to take `mttaSecs`, the mean seconds to recover `mttrSecs` and the escalation rate `escalationRate`. |

**`response.current` and `response.previous`**

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `incidents` | The incidents created in the period. |
| `responded` | The incidents someone took. Taking one is the first acknowledgement; one resolved by a person without being acknowledged counts at the moment it was resolved. Incidents whose rule recovered on its own do not count. |
| `mtta` | Seconds from the trigger to the first time someone took the incident: the mean `meanSecs`, the median `p50Secs` and `p90Secs`. All `0` when no one took any. |
| `mttr` | Seconds from the trigger to recovery over the recovered incidents, with the same mean, P50 and P90. |
| `escalationRate` | The share from 0 to 1 of incidents that escalated: those that reached the second step or were [escalated now](/en-US/api/alerting/escalate-incident). |
| `steps` | How each step did, `step` counting from 0: the incidents that reached it, `reached`, and the ones acknowledged at it, `acknowledged`. Only incidents with escalations on their timeline count; empty when the period has none. |
| `ackVia` | Where the incidents were first taken: `web`, `api`, `im`, `sms`, `phone` or `agent`, with a count each. |
