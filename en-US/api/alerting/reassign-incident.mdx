title: "Reassign an incident"
description: "Give an incident to people, a team, or an escalation policy."
"zh-Hans_link": "/zh-Hans/api/alerting/reassign-incident"
---

```http
POST /api/v1/alerts/incidents/{id}/reassign
```

Gives an active incident to someone else. The body names the new owner with `kind`:

- `users`: the people in `userIds`, 1 to 10 members of the organization, become the assignees and
  receive an escalation notification whose `escalation.reason` is `reassigned`. The incident goes back to
  `open`, and its current step starts its ack timeout again; when nobody acknowledges, escalation goes on
  to the next step.
- `team`: the team in `teamId` takes the incident, which moves to the team's on-call policy, or to the
  policy named `default` when the team has none, and starts again from its first step that applies.
- `policy`: the incident moves to the policy in `escalationPolicyId` and starts again from its first
  step that applies; the team stays.

The incident goes back to `open` in each case, a snooze ends, and the step is recorded on the
[timeline](/en-US/api/alerting/incident-timeline). The call requires `alerts.incidents.update`.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kind` | string | Yes | `users`, `team`, or `policy`. |
| `userIds` | array | With `users` | 1 to 10 user IDs of members of the organization. |
| `teamId` | string | With `team` | The team. |
| `escalationPolicyId` | string | With `policy` | The policy. |

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST http://localhost:5080/api/v1/alerts/incidents/$INCIDENT_ID/reassign \
  -H "authorization: Bearer $MS_TOKEN" \
  -H "content-type: application/json" \
  -d '{"kind": "users", "userIds": ["<user-id>"]}'
```
</RequestExample>

The response is the updated incident. [List incidents](/en-US/api/alerting/list-incidents) describes every field.

**Errors**

| Status | Cause |
|---|---|
| `400` | `userIds` is empty, has more than 10 users, or names a user outside the organization; or the team has no policy and there is no `default` policy; or no step of the policy applies to the incident's severity. |
| `403` | The token lacks `alerts.incidents.update`, or the incident is out of the token's reach. |
| `404` | The incident does not exist. |
| `409` | The incident has recovered or was merged into another, or it kept changing while it was being updated; retry. |
