---
title: "Add responders to an incident"
description: "Add people to an incident's assignees and notify them."
"zh-Hans_link": "/zh-Hans/api/alerting/add-incident-responders"
---

```http
POST /api/v1/alerts/incidents/{id}/responders
```

Adds the people in `user_ids` to the assignees of an active incident and sends each newcomer an escalation
notification whose `escalation.reason` is `responder_added`. The status of the incident stays as it is,
and people who are already assignees are not notified again. The call requires `alerts.incidents.update`.

**Body**

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `user_ids` | array | Yes | 1 to 10 user IDs of members of the organization. |

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST http://localhost:5080/api/v1/alerts/incidents/$INCIDENT_ID/responders \
  -H "authorization: Bearer $MS_TOKEN" \
  -H "content-type: application/json" \
  -d '{"user_ids": ["<another-user-id>"]}'
```
</RequestExample>

The response is the updated incident. [List incidents](/en-US/api/alerting/list-incidents) describes every field.

**Errors**

| Status | Cause |
|---|---|
| `400` | `user_ids` is empty, has more than 10 users, or names a user outside the organization. |
| `403` | The token lacks `alerts.incidents.update`, or the incident is out of the token's reach. |
| `404` | The incident does not exist. |
| `409` | The incident has recovered or was merged into another, or it kept changing while it was being updated; retry. |
