```http
POST /api/v1/prometheus/api/v1/write
```

Accepts Prometheus and VictoriaMetrics `remote_write` version 1 requests, which are snappy-compressed protobuf. The call requires `streams.write` and answers `204` on success.

A request is written to the metrics stream named by the `stream-name` header, `default` when the header is absent. A metrics stream holds the metrics of one source. The `__name__` label becomes the `metric_name` field, the remaining labels become fields, and the sample becomes the `value` field. A label named like a metric field, such as `metric_name` or `metric_kind`, is kept as `exported_<name>`. Native histograms and metric metadata are skipped, and exemplars are stored beside the samples.

<RequestExample>
```yaml prometheus.yml
remote_write:
  - url: http://localhost:5080/api/v1/prometheus/api/v1/write
    authorization:
      credentials: "${MS_TOKEN}"
    headers:
      stream-name: prod
```
</RequestExample>

Query the metrics with PromQL by their names, whichever stream holds them. A series of `http_requests_total` with the labels `service` and `status` becomes:

```json
{
  "columns": ["value", "service", "status"],
  "rows": [[25.0, "checkout", "200"]],
  "scanned_rows": 1,
  "took_ms": 154
}
```

**Limits** apply per series and per organization: at most 64 labels per series, label names up to 128 bytes, label values up to 2048 bytes, and cardinality limits for active and new series. The process limits are `[intake.prometheus]` in [Configuration](/en-US/configuration#intake). The active series of an organization and of one of its metrics are limited by the platform setting **Settings → Metric cardinality**, read with `GET /api/v1/settings/series_limits` and set with `PUT` and the same body, `{ "max_active_series_per_org": 200000, "max_active_series_per_metric": 100000 }`, both with `sys.settings.manage` in the system scope. The metric limit is at most the organization limit, and both are at most 100,000,000.
