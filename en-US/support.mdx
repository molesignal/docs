## Report a bug

A report that includes these details is easier to reproduce:

- The version from `molesignal --version`, and the commit from `GET /api/v1/version`.
- The deployment type: Docker Compose, binary, or Kubernetes, and the roles that run.
- The relevant part of the configuration, with secrets removed.
- The log lines around the failure.
- The steps that trigger the problem, the expected result, and the actual result.

Never paste tokens, passwords, `MS_CIPHER_KEY`, or database connection strings into a public issue.

## Report a security vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Use one of the private channels:

- [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/molesignal/molesignal/security/advisories/new).
- [security@molesignal.com](mailto:security@molesignal.com). A PGP key is available on request.

Include a short description of the impact, a minimal reproduction, the version or commit that was tested, and a suggested severity.

The maintainers acknowledge a report within 3 business days and triage it within 7 business days. A fix or a mitigation plan for a high or critical issue follows within 30 days. Disclosure is coordinated, and reporters are credited in the release notes unless they ask to stay anonymous.

While MoleSignal is pre-1.0, security fixes go to the `main` branch and the latest tagged release. The `alpha` and `beta` channels receive forward fixes only.
