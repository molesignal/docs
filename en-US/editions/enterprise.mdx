title: "Enterprise Edition"
description: "Self-host MoleSignal with signed commercial entitlements and vendor support."
---

Enterprise Edition runs the same self-hosted engine as OpenSource Edition and adds capabilities from
a verified, active signed license. The license contains explicit feature names and limits; an
Enterprise deployment should not assume every commercial entitlement is present.

## What Enterprise adds

Depending on the contract and signed license, Enterprise can enable:

- Mole Agent, governed Tools, automation, and Inbound MCP through `agent`;
- SSO provider management through `sso`;
- remote-cluster registration and federated query through `federated_search`;
- organization domain management through `domain_management`;
- enhanced profiling comparison workflows through `profiling_enhanced`;
- commercial limits or deployment-specific capabilities included in the signed package.

Enterprise engagements can also include priority support, response commitments, security patch
backports, architecture review, onboarding, and compliance assistance. Contract terms—not this
documentation—are authoritative for support obligations.

## Security model

License entitlements and IAM permissions are independent gates. For example, enabling `agent` does
not grant `agent.use`, `agent.manage`, or `agent.approve`. The request must pass organization scope,
permission, resource relationship, policy, and license checks.

## Activate Enterprise

Use [Enterprise license management](/en-US/licensing) to upload and activate an immutable signed
license version. System-scoped license operations require `sys.licenses.read` or
`sys.licenses.manage`.

For commercial evaluation, visit the [Enterprise page](https://www.molesignal.io/enterprise).
